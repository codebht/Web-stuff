01 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

A better future
starts at the village level.

UDAAN EK GAON KI shows how simple, affordable ideas can help make rural communities greener, safer and better connected.

Our prototype combines four important areas of rural development in one village model. It uses accessible technology, sustainable practices and practical demonstrations to explain how these ideas can work together.

02 / THE FOUR PILLARS

Four ideas. One village.

Each part of our model represents one important need of a rural community.

01
☀

Rural Connectivity & Offline Information

Solar energy and existing smartphones can help people access useful information even where internet connectivity is limited.

  • Solar energy utilization
  • Offline educational resources
  • Healthcare and government information
02
♧

Land Desertification & Soil Restoration

Healthy soil is important for farming and the environment. This section demonstrates methods that support soil restoration.

  • Mycorrhizal fungal inoculant
  • Compost and soil samples
  • Native seeds and plants
03
⌁

Climate Disaster Monitoring

An Arduino-compatible system demonstrates how sensors can monitor environmental conditions and provide alerts.

  • Soil-moisture and water-level sensors
  • Temperature and humidity sensors
  • LED indicators and buzzer alerts
04
＋

Education & Healthcare Access

Learning resources and healthcare awareness should be easier to access, including in remote areas.

  • Village school and digital education centre
  • Offline learning resources
  • Mobile mini-hospital concept
03 / THE PROTOTYPE

Small in size.
Big in purpose.

A compact model designed to explain how the four pillars connect.

The prototype is planned for a base area of 120.1 cm × 60.1 cm. Recycled and cost-effective materials are used wherever possible, and every component has a purpose.
